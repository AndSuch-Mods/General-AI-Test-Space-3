Resolve First

Three items that can change the entire quote

AB hardware compatibility

The package specifies 5034 PointMax I/O, while the Hot/Coldwell photo appears to show an older 1769 CompactLogix. Confirm whether a controller upgrade or different remote I/O platform is intended.

GE / PROFINET compatibility

The package specifies a Weidmüller PROFINET coupler, while the photos show legacy GE Series 90-30 and Genius equipment. Confirm the actual PROFINET controller, gateway, or upgrade plan.

Meter output

Verify every Seametrics meter has a usable 4–20 mA output and the required passive-loop power/output cable. Do not assume the wireless system is presently using analog.
